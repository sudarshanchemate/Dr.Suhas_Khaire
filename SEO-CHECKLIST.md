# SEO checklist – drsuhaskhaire.com

## Done on the website
- Keyword-focused title, description and H1 on every page (doctor name, specialty, Kharadi/Pune)
- 5 dedicated treatment pages: diabetes, thyroid, PCOS/hormones, obesity, child growth & puberty
- FAQ sections on the home page and every treatment page, with FAQ schema
- Structured data: MedicalClinic + Physician + WebSite (home), MedicalWebPage + Breadcrumb (treatment pages)
- Address, phone, hours, map coordinates and "areas served" (Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Wagholi, Hadapsar…)
- sitemap.xml, robots.txt, canonical URLs, Open Graph preview image for WhatsApp/Facebook shares
- Images compressed to WebP (about 70% smaller), descriptive alt text, mobile-friendly layout

## Domain
All canonical/schema URLs use **https://drsuhaskhaire.com/**. If the site goes on a different domain,
find-and-replace `https://drsuhaskhaire.com/` across all .html files, sitemap.xml and robots.txt.

## After launch (needed to rank – cannot be done from the code)
1. **Google Search Console:** verify the domain, submit `sitemap.xml`, request indexing of all 6 pages.
2. **Google Business Profile (most important for "near me" / area searches):**
   - Set the website link to the new site
   - Fix the office number so it matches everywhere (104 on Google vs 106 on posters)
   - Categories: Endocrinologist (primary), Diabetologist, Pediatric endocrinologist
   - Add services, upload fresh photos regularly, post updates weekly
   - Keep asking happy patients for reviews, and reply to every review
3. **Same name, address and phone everywhere** (NAP): Practo, Justdial, Lybrate, MediBuddy, Sulekha,
   Apollo 247, Facebook, Instagram. Add the website link on each.
4. **Links:** Noble Hospital doctor profile, the Indian Express article and the Zee 24 Taas feature
   should link to the website if possible.
5. **Test:** https://search.google.com/test/rich-results and https://pagespeed.web.dev after hosting.
6. **Keep content fresh:** one short article a month (for example "HbA1c explained", "Thyroid in pregnancy").

Expect results in 2–4 months. Searching the doctor's name usually ranks quickly; competitive terms like
"endocrinologist in Pune" take longer and depend mostly on reviews and the Google Business Profile.
