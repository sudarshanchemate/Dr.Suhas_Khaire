About the Doctor

Dr. Suhas Khaire

MBBS, MD, DM (Endocrinology) · Consultant Endocrinologist & Diabetologist

Dr. Suhas Khaire holds a DM in Endocrinology, the highest qualification in the field of diabetes, thyroid and hormonal disorders, from the prestigious BYL Nair Hospital & Topiwala National Medical College, Mumbai.

At Harmony, he treats the full range of endocrine conditions in adults and children, from diabetes and thyroid disease to PCOS, infertility, growth and bone disorders. Patients value that he takes time to listen, explains the condition clearly to them and their family, and builds a practical plan for medication, diet and follow-up.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What does an endocrinologist treat?

An endocrinologist specialises in hormone-related conditions such as diabetes, thyroid disorders, PCOS, obesity, osteoporosis, adrenal and pituitary disorders, and growth or puberty problems in children.

Where is Dr. Suhas Khaire's clinic in Pune?

Harmony Diabetes, Thyroid & Endocrine Centre is at Office No. 106, Nyati Eureka, Mundhwa–Kharadi Road, next to Manipal Hospital, Kharadi, Pune 411014. It is easily reachable from Viman Nagar, Wagholi, Chandan Nagar, Wadgaon Sheri, Mundhwa, Magarpatta and Hadapsar.

What are the clinic timings?

Monday to Friday 4:00–8:00 PM and Saturday 3:00–8:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays.

How do I book an appointment?

Call or WhatsApp +91 77700 13575, or fill in the appointment form on this page. The clinic will confirm your time slot.

Does Dr. Khaire treat children?

Yes. He sees children for growth problems, short stature, early or delayed puberty, childhood diabetes and thyroid disorders.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring your previous blood reports, prescriptions and the medicines you are currently taking. If you check your sugar at home, bring your glucometer readings too.
